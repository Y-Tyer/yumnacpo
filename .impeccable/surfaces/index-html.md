## Direction contract

THESIS: One line-drawn woman dances down the page; each pose points at a body region and the products for it appear beside her. Refuses the clinic template of portrait hero plus a grid of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Lilac-white ground, deep plum and plum sections, marigold, sage and blush tiles behind cut-out flat-colour product illustrations, small pill labels with cursor arrows, hand-drawn underlines and sparkles. Young Serif display in two tones, Figtree text.

STORY: The visitor sees who Yumna is, finds their part of the body, sees what support exists for it, and messages her.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo large at the top, mission line in display serif beneath it, WhatsApp button and phone link, then the portrait on a marigold tile with two floating labels. On desktop the portrait sits to the right.

SIGNATURE: Pinned services stage. Scrolling advances the figure through eight poses, playing a short generated clip between neighbouring poses, while an in-place product card loops beside her with a vertical progress bar.
