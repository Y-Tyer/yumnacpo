# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS/JS (user's choice). No framework or build step. Deploy target not yet specified.

## Users

- **Potential patients**: people considering orthotic or prosthetic support who want to understand what is offered and what daily life looks like with it.
- **Referrers**: healthcare professionals who refer patients to the practitioner and need to judge services and products quickly.

## Product Purpose

A personal professional site for an orthotist-prosthetist. After browsing, a visitor should understand which services and products are offered, and see what support looks like with and without them. Visitors may also enquire or contact the practitioner.

## Positioning

A single practitioner's own site, not a clinic directory or product catalogue. It combines the practitioner's services with real social content (tagged Instagram and YouTube posts from the practitioner and partnering practitioners) as additional information.

## Operating Context

- Content is clinical and personal, so trust, clarity, and dignity matter to both audiences.
- Social content is pulled in from Instagram and YouTube, tagged to the practitioner or to partners. The embedding mechanism on a static site is undecided.
- As of November 2026 the Instagram account (`@yumnacpo`) is live and there is no YouTube channel yet. No educational content per body region exists yet; those areas are placeholders.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Presents services and products, with a with/without-support comparison.
- Shows tagged Instagram and YouTube content.
- Lets visitors enquire or contact the practitioner. Email, contact details, and socials are supplied. Whether there is a form, and how it is handled on a static host, is undecided.
- Name: the project is called "yumnacpo". The practitioner's full name and credentials are to be supplied by the user. Do not infer them.
- Undecided: the list of services and products, the exact comparison format, and the hosting target.

## Brand Commitments

A logo exists and will be supplied. It is binding and must be preserved. No other brand constraints were stated.

## Evidence on Hand

To be supplied by the user: photo, bio, credentials, logo, email address, contact details, social links. None are in the project yet. Do not fabricate testimonials, patient stories, outcomes, credentials, prices, or clinical claims. Use clearly marked placeholders until real material arrives.

## Product Principles

1. Make clear who this is for: a patient and a referrer need different things, and both should find them fast.
2. Show the difference support makes, with real material, rather than describing it in the abstract.
3. Credibility comes from real credentials, real practitioners, and real content. Never invent them.
4. Contact should be easy and low-pressure for people who may be in a vulnerable situation.
5. Make no clinical claim that the practitioner has not supplied or confirmed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visitors may have limb difference, mobility limitations, or other disability, so accessibility is a core requirement, not an extra. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA or better. That means strong contrast, large touch targets, keyboard and screen-reader support, captions or transcripts for video, and reduced-motion support. Alt text on imagery of people should be respectful and descriptive.
